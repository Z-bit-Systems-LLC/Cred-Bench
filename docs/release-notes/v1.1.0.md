## Cred-Bench 1.1.0

This release lets you get out of a scan that is stuck on an unresponsive reader, shows PKOC and LEAF Verified credentials in the format your access control system expects, and adds an About dialog.

### New

- A Cancel button appears while a card is being scanned. A reader that stops responding no longer leaves the app on "Scanning Card..." for good, and if the reader stays stuck the status suggests unplugging it. Card removal and Reset Reader no longer hang on a stuck reader either.
- The PKOC tab has a Format drop-down next to the credential size. One copyable box shows the credential as Wiegand bits, hex or decimal.
- The LEAF Verified tab shows the W40-2 frame as Wiegand bits, hex or decimal through the same Format drop-down. Decimal is the whole 40-bit frame, parity bits included.
- An About button on the scan panel opens a dialog with the version, copyright and the licenses that Cred-Bench and its third-party libraries ship under.

### Fixes

- When a newly scanned card has no tab for the technology that was selected for the previous card, the details now switch to the General tab instead of staying on a hidden tab.

### Changes

- The ISO14443 tab no longer shows the card serial number (CSN). Any reader can read it without authentication and it is easily cloned, so the tab now warns against using it as a credential identifier. The CLI still reports it.
- The Cred-Bench logo now appears in the title bar of the main window and the Program Card dialog.
